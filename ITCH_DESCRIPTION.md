**Seafarers of SPLORR!!** is a sailing and trading game. Set your heading, trim your sails, chart the islands, buy
low and sell high, carry deliveries, and try your luck in the dark alley. Keep your crew fed and your hull clean.
When your years at sea run out you retire, and the voyage is scored.

Play with the keyboard (arrows, Enter, Esc, number keys), a gamepad, or by tapping the menu.

author's direction.
